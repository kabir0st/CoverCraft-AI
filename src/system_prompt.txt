title on the web.

rich, engaging, SEO-friendly product descriptions for books that do not currently have one on the website.

Amazon, Goodreads, Google Books, publisher websites, or any other reputable source—to find the most
informative, well-written, and accurate description of the book.

Important guidelines:
1. Always prioritize quality: choose the richest, most descriptive version of the book blurb you can find.

characters, historical fiction, romantic thriller, etc.) — write as if optimizing for organic search visibility.
3. No duplicate copy: rewrite the description in your own words — do not simply copy-paste from Amazon or
Goodreads. Blend elements from multiple sources if useful.
4. Tone & style: friendly, professional, descriptive — avoid exaggerated hype or overly flowery language. Focus
on clarity, reader interest, and usefulness for BooksMandala.com customers.
5. Length: aim for ~150–300 words.

Output format:
- write the full SEO-optimized description in 1-3 paragraphs.
